# JoshBot Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

JoshBot is intended to be a respectful and useful open-source project.

Contributors, maintainers, and participants are expected to help keep project
spaces welcoming, professional, and safe regardless of experience, identity,
background, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Examples of positive participation include:

- communicating clearly and respectfully;
- criticizing ideas and code without attacking people;
- accepting constructive feedback;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them;
- respecting privacy and reasonable boundaries;
- keeping reports relevant to the project;
- helping other participants understand technical decisions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or discrimination;
- insults or personal attacks;
- sexualized language or unwanted attention;
- deliberate disruption of issues, reviews, or discussions;
- publishing another person’s private information;
- impersonation or deceptive attribution;
- repeatedly ignoring a maintainer’s reasonable direction;
- retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to JoshBot repository spaces, including issues,
pull requests, reviews, and other project-managed discussions.

It also applies when someone publicly represents the project or its community.

## Reporting a concern

Open a
[conduct report](https://github.com/joshternet/joshbot/issues/new?template=conduct_report.yml).

Conduct issues are public. Do not include private personal information,
credentials, private messages, home addresses, phone numbers, or other
sensitive material.

JoshBot does not provide an email-based or private project conduct-reporting
channel. If the problem cannot be described safely in public and it violates
GitHub’s platform rules, use GitHub’s own content-reporting tools on the
relevant account, comment, issue, or repository.

Security vulnerabilities are different. Report them privately under
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not through the conduct form.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that violates this
policy. The maintainer may also warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently
exclude participants when necessary to protect the project.

Enforcement decisions will consider:

- the seriousness and impact of the behavior;
- whether the behavior was intentional or repeated;
- whether the participant accepts responsibility;
- whether a proportionate correction can protect the community.
